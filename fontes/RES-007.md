## Trechos extraídos (literais)
- Science: Brazil averaged 409 PISA points (OECD: 482)
- Mathematics: Brazil averaged 377 PISA points (OECD: 463)
- Reading: Brazil averaged 408 PISA points (OECD: 461)
- Computational Problem Solving: 406 (OECD average: 500); Environmental Science: 413 (OECD average: 480)
- "Between 2022 and 2025, science performance in Brazil changed by 6 PISA score points" (not statistically significant)
- Mathematics (2022-2025): -2 points (not statistically significant)
- Reading (2022-2025): -2 points (not statistically significant)
- "over its longer period of participation in PISA, the average decennial rate of change in science performance was 7 PISA score points, a trend that was not statistically significant."
- Low performers (Science): 52.4% Brazil vs 25.7% OECD average; Top performers (Science, Level 5-6): 0.9% vs 7.2% OECD.
